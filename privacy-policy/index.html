Privacy policy.

What information we collect through this website, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Safeway Roofing & Chimneys (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides roofing, guttering, chimney and related exterior services across Dublin, Kildare, Wicklow and surrounding areas. We are the data controller for personal information collected through this website.

Contact: Safeway Roofing & Chimneys, 51 Bracken Road, Dublin, D18 CV48. Phone: 087 485 8556. Email: safewayroofing.chimneys@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, phone number, area and a description of the work you need, when you use our quote form, call, email or message us.
  • Project information: photos, measurements, inspection notes and job details created while providing a quote or service.
  • Technical data: the page you sent a form from and, if you arrived from an advert, the advert reference (for example a Google click ID). With your consent, cookies also record device and usage information.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote
  • To schedule, carry out and manage work you have booked, including emergency call-outs
  • To issue invoices and guarantees and keep accounting records
  • To contact you about your project, including follow-up and aftercare
  • To understand which pages and adverts lead to enquiries, where you have consented
  • To meet our legal, tax and insurance obligations

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

4. Legal basis for processing

Under data protection law (including the GDPR) we rely on: contract (to provide a quote or carry out work you request), legitimate interests (to run and secure our business and respond to enquiries), legal obligation (accounting, tax and insurance records) and consent (for optional cookies). Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

5. Quote forms

Details you submit through our forms are sent to us by email through a form delivery provider (Web3Forms) so we can respond. We use them only to deal with your request and to follow up on it.

6. WhatsApp and phone contact

If you contact us by phone or WhatsApp, we keep the details you share (such as your number and messages) only to respond and manage your enquiry or project. WhatsApp is operated by a third party and is subject to its own privacy policy.

7. Cookies and advertising

This website does not need cookies to work. If you accept the cookie notice, Google cookies are used to measure visits and to tell us when an advert led to an enquiry. If you decline, those cookies are not set. You can change your mind by clearing this site’s data in your browser. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the map on our contact page is provided by Google Maps; both are subject to Google’s privacy policy.

8. Sharing your information

We only share personal data with trusted providers who help us run our business, for example website hosting, email and form services, accountants and insurers, and where we are required to by law. Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area; where this happens we take reasonable steps to make sure appropriate safeguards are in place.

9. Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes above. Enquiry details are kept for as long as needed to deal with your request and for a reasonable period afterwards. Job, invoice and guarantee records are kept for the periods required by tax, accounting and warranty rules.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, have inaccurate data corrected, have your data deleted, restrict or object to our processing, receive your data in a portable format and withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details above. We will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your personal data. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Children

Our website and services are directed at adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.

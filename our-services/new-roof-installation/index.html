New Roof Installation

New Roofs done properly.

A new roof is one of the biggest investments you can make in your home. Safeway Roofing & Chimneys fits long-lasting, weatherproof roofs using quality materials chosen to handle Irish conditions in Dublin, Kildare and Wicklow.

Whether you are replacing an old roof or covering a new extension, our crew works safely and carefully from start to finish. We install pitched and flat roofs, slate and tile finishes, and UPVC fascia and soffits to complete the job.

Common questions

New Roofs: your questions answered.

How much does a new roof cost?

The price depends on the roof’s size, materials and style. Get in touch for a clear quote based on your home.

Do you fit both pitched and flat roofs?

Yes. We install pitched and flat roofs, with slate and tile finishes, and can fit UPVC fascia and soffits to complete the job.

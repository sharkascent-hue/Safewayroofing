Meet Safeway

Roofing & guttering help right when your home needs it.

Safeway Roofing & Chimneys is a local roofing and chimney specialist covering Dublin, Kildare and Wicklow, with expertise in roofs, gutters and chimney repairs.

Our insured, experienced team handles new roofs, repairs, flat roofing, UPVC fascia and soffits, guttering and chimney work, always aiming for sturdy, weatherproof results that look great. Beyond the roof itself, we also take on ridge tile repointing, brick cleaning and repointing, and interior and exterior painting and decorating.

Skilled & insured

Our insured, experienced crew delivers high-standard roofing, guttering and chimney work.

Quick to respond

From sudden leaks to planned upgrades, we arrive on time and finish the job properly.

Common questions

Good to know.

How can I tell if my chimney needs repair?

Look for cracked bricks, crumbling mortar, leaks, damaged flashing or unusual smoke behaviour. Regular checks catch problems early.

Will chimney repairs stop leaks and water damage?

Yes. Properly sealed flashing, brickwork and mortar keep water out and protect your home.

How long does a chimney repair take?

Smaller repairs can often be finished in a day, while bigger structural work may take a few days. We keep disruption to a minimum.

Is your chimney work insured?

Yes. Safeway Roofing & Chimneys is fully insured and every job is carried out by skilled professionals.

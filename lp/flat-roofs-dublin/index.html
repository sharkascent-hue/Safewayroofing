Common questions

Good to know.

How long does a flat roof last?

A well-installed flat roof can last 15 to 30 years depending on the materials and how well it is looked after. We use durable membranes for lasting protection.

Are flat roofs prone to leaks?

Not when they are installed and maintained properly. Correct waterproofing, good drainage and quality materials keep water out.

Does a flat roof need regular maintenance?

Yes. An annual check for debris, blocked outlets or small damage helps extend its life and avoids costly repairs.

Which flat roof systems do you fit?

We fit felt, fibreglass and single-ply rubber membrane roofs, and recommend the best option for your property and budget.

Common questions

Good to know.

How can I tell if my roof needs repairing?

Watch for missing or cracked tiles, damp patches or stains inside, sagging sections and damaged flashing. A regular inspection helps catch problems early.

Can you repair every type of roof?

Yes. We repair pitched, flat, slate, tile and UPVC roofing across Dublin, Kildare and Wicklow.

How long will a repair take?

Small repairs are often finished in a day. Larger jobs can take two to three days depending on the damage.

Is your repair work insured?

Yes. All work is fully insured and carried out by trained professionals.

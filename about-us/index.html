Who we are

Tidy, careful work on every job.

Safeway Roofing & Chimneys looks after roofs, gutters and chimneys for homeowners in Dublin, Kildare and Wicklow.

Our insured crew takes pride in tidy, careful work on every job, from new roofs and repairs to flat roofing, UPVC gutters, fascia and soffits, and chimney work.

We also handle ridge tile repointing, brick cleaning and repointing, and painting and decorating inside and out.

Every quote is clear and upfront, so you know exactly where you stand before we start.

Our aim

Keep your home dry, safe and well looked after.

Our aim at Safeway Roofing & Chimneys is simple: keep your home dry, safe and well looked after.
